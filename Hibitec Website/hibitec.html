Selected Work

Ideas we've taken to production.

A look at how we approach real client problems — from first concept through to a working, maintained product.

WhatsApp AI Concierge · Full-Stack Build

Fezzy's Beauty Studio

Challenge

A growing beauty studio needed a way to handle bookings and customer questions on WhatsApp without hiring extra front-desk staff.

Solution

We built a hybrid rule-based and AI concierge backend on FastAPI, integrated with the WhatsApp Business API, alongside a fully redesigned booking-friendly website with refined scroll animation.

Value

Customers get instant answers and bookings around the clock, while the studio runs its front-of-house with a fraction of the manual effort.

Brand & Web Platform

Vertex Forge Technologies

Challenge

A technical services brand needed a site that felt precise and engineered, not generic — reflecting the drafting and blueprint discipline of its work.

Solution

A full agency website built around a blueprint / drafting visual language: structured grids, technical typography, and a confident, understated palette.

Value

A digital front door that signals technical credibility before a single conversation happens.

Business Management Platform

Encryption Hub

Challenge

An IT services venture needed a coherent platform to manage its business operations alongside a formal plan to guide its growth.

Solution

A full business-management platform paired with a structured business plan document, giving the founders one system to run and present the company.

Value

Operations and strategy live in one place, ready to scale as the business grows.

AI-Powered Web App

Budget & Expense Tracker

Challenge

Everyday budgeting tools rarely feel built for South African users, or make it easy to actually understand spending patterns.

Solution

A Node.js and Claude-powered tracker with a dark, ambient interface, built natively around ZAR and everyday local spending categories.

Value

A genuinely usable financial tool, and a proof point for Hibitec's ability to embed intelligence directly into a product.
